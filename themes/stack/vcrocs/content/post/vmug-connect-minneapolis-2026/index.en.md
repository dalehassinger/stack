**VMUG Connect 2026:**

[VMUG Connect 2026 in Minneapolis](https://connect.vmug.com/minneapolis/2026/content) is the first that I will be presenting outside of my local VMUG or VMware Explore. I am looking forward to the event to share my vCommunity story.

### The Path to Influence: From VMUG Attendee to vExpert, Blogger, and Conference Speaker

**Session Details:**
If you've ever felt the urge to present at a VMUG or start a technical blog but hesitated, this session is for you. At my first VMUG event, I was asking myself, "Can I do this?" My story is about setting a goal and finding fulfillment in the vCommunity by operating under one simple metric: If even a single person learns something new, the effort was worthwhile. Hear the personal story behind my decision to give back, the steps I took, and how you can start your own journey of presenting and contributing.  

---

My presentation schedule for VMUG Connect Minneapolis is finalized. I am involved in the following presentations and co-presentations during the conference.  

Consider attending "VMware Hands-On Lab: Monitoring Your Private Cloud Platform with VCF Operations (HOL-2610-03)" if you have not participated in an Expert-Led Workshop (ELW). While we use the HOL as a foundation, the session is driven by attendee questions and interests. We will explore features and configurations not covered in the standard lab guide. 👍🏻
